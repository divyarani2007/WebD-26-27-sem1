About Us

ABES Engineering College is a private autonomous engineering institute located in Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR, India. Established in 2000, it is affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
